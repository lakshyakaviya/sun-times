Sunrise and sunset are checked against published almanac times, and against physical identities that any correct implementation has to satisfy — the sun being on the horizon at the moment sunrise is claimed, equinox days lasting about 12h07m everywhere, solar noon staying within the equation of time of mean noon. Tolerances are stated per check, and each row reports the value actually measured, so a check that passes by luck is still visible.
